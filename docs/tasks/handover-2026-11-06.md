# Handover — I2 prototype (scope card assistant)

Written so a fresh conversation can resume the prototype work without reconstructing the one that
produced this note. Scope: the prototype only. Design-system and tooling questions were settled
separately and are deliberately not covered here.

Branch `claude/optimistic-thompson-0mwtyb`. Acceptance criteria already exist in
[candidate-scope-card-assistant.md](candidate-scope-card-assistant.md) — read that file first; this
note says where the work stands and what stops it.

## What we are building

The supersprint `tydzien-2` decision, approved by Justyna on 2026-10-30: build a **clickable
prototype of idea I2** and keep researching. Explicitly not a rollout — whether it ships depends on
experiment E2, which measures payment for the next job ad and needs the real DoRoboty team and 3-4
months. In this case E2 stays a plan.

Two screens:

1. **Candidate.** The assistant asks about a vague answer — "Co dokladnie zrobiles sam? Jaki byl
   efekt? Skad wiesz, ze zadzialalo?" — and composes a card with a fixed structure, _Moja czesc /
   Co robil zespol / Efekt / Jak to sprawdzono_, in the candidate's own words. The candidate can
   ask the assistant what a question means, and approves or corrects the card before it is sent.
2. **Recruiter.** The approved card sits at the top of the application, with a line counting the
   concrete facts in it ("3 konkrety: ...") or saying there are none.

Safeguards that are part of the idea, not decoration: the exchange is written, never an avatar; the
candidate approves the card; "ja" and "my" stay as the candidate wrote them.

**Course deadline: about 2026-11-23.** The prototype is what the deadline needs.

## Why this and not something else

Outcome under test: companies that bought in month M make a new paid purchase within 90 days, 30%
-> 60%, with ~45% as the first milestone. The drop-off sits between the draft of the next ad and
payment, 49% -> 35%. Opportunity O01 — the recruiter cannot tell from an application what the
candidate did themselves — scored highest (PRES 16) and is the only thread that changed in June
2026, when Match Score rose while continuations after interviews did not.

The mechanism I2 bets on: applications with specifics in one shared structure stop sounding alike,
so the recruiter spends less effort, invites fewer candidates "just to check", and is more willing
to pay for the next ad.

Two caveats are in Justyna's own documents and should shape the build:

- **S20** undermined the simple version of I2 — merely keeping one sentence about the candidate's
  own work changed nothing. The new form (asking plus the card) has not been checked against data.
- **S18** suggests the draft timing is a system rule, not a company decision, which weakens the
  "80% of companies start a draft" figure that the draft-to-payment framing rests on.

So build the prototype so its central claim can fail visibly. The verification step below is that
test and it must be able to come out negative.

## What the application already provides

- Candidate application form with a free-text answer, length validation, draft save to localStorage
  and consent — `src/features/prototype/workspace.tsx`.
- Recruiter view of an application with answer/CV tabs, match score, process history and summary —
  `recruiting-application.tsx` and `application-record.tsx`. The card has an obvious home at the top
  of that record.
- Legacy model fields `sourceNotes`, `generatedAnswer` and `answer`, left over from a removed AI
  flow, so a raw-versus-final slot exists with no UI behind it.

## What has to be built

The assistant exchange, the four-field card, the candidate approval step and the recruiter's
fact-count line. None of it exists yet.

- Store the card in a **new** field rather than reusing the legacy ones, so version-1 saves and
  imports keep their current meaning and still import.
- **The prototype has no backend and makes no external requests**, so the assistant must be
  deterministic and local: scripted prompts, rule-based extraction. The fact counter is a heuristic
  and the UI has to label it as a prototype signal, not a score. That is enough for a clickable
  prototype, but it must not look like working AI.
- Declining the assistant stays possible and must not block submitting an application.
- Preserve role gates, hash URLs, shared Zod schemas, draft save and resume, answer and consent
  validation and focus, and immutable submitted records.

Only the first one actually stops implementation. The other two can be decided while building.
